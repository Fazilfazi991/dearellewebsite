# The Dearelle Edit — concept film production

Source platform: Google Flow / Veo. Project: https://flow.google.com/project/2df5e297-04b9-4ee1-b458-5a5faa166d47

The user selected a second account for the final take: https://flow.google.com/u/1/project/b8d78570-00d4-45e9-af69-b26250d79ca1

## Product truth
No verified Dearelle product photographs exist in this prototype. The existing catalogue uses stock imagery. The campaign therefore uses fictional, unbranded accessories and must be identified as a concept film, never as real inventory.

The owner requested removal of visible concept text and the campaign title from the hero. Provenance is recorded here; the visible video contains no title or concept label. The shopping CTA is below the footage, and the owner-supplied logo appears in header/footer.

## Reference
`source/dearelle-concept-reference.jpg` was generated in Flow with Nano Banana 2.1. Adult South Asian model, ivory blazer and cream top, dark low bun, chocolate structured top-handle bag with simple gold clasp, slim gold watch with champagne dial. Warm Mediterranean architecture, natural skin texture and daylight. No titles or invented logos.

## Edit intent
Three actual source takes: arrival (Veo 3.1 Fast, portrait, 20 credits), watch detail (Veo 3.1 Fast, landscape, 20 credits), signature portrait (Veo 3.1 Lite, landscape, 10 credits). The mobile edit uses all three takes: 3 seconds arrival, 2.8 seconds detail, 3 seconds signature, with brief dissolves and a return to the opening frame. The desktop edit uses the full landscape signature and detail footage, with different signature moments for opening/closing. No stretched portrait footage or artificial side panels. Exports are approximately 8.4 seconds, silent H.264, 24fps; 720x1280 mobile and 1280x720 desktop.

## Budget boundary
50 credits total across both accounts. No purchases, upgrades or top-ups. The watch-detail attempt failed with an audio error and was refunded: balance was checked at 30 before the successful retry. Successful Fast takes cost 40 credits net on the first account. The final Lite take cost 10 on the user-selected second account, whose balance was checked at 40 afterward. Reference image generation was free. No more generation is authorized under this cap.

## Status
All three source MP4s exported and inspected through sampled-frame contact sheets. The reference and original files are in source/. Final masters, optimized MP4s and WebP posters are generated by `node media/hero/render.mjs` with FFmpeg. `render-report.json` records the actual output sizes. The earlier two-take fallback script is retained as production history and is not used by the storefront.

## Quality limits
This is an AI concept film, not verified product cinematography. No obvious extra digits, disappearing accessories or clothing changes appeared in the sampled selected moments. Dial markings, bracelet finish and tiny hardware details can still vary between generated takes. The loop uses an editorial dissolve rather than claiming a physically seamless camera move. Mobile closing footage is cropped from the landscape source and therefore has less detail than the native portrait take. These limitations remain subject to owner review; no claim of real inventory accuracy is made.
