All nine product stills and the category banner were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Prompts are in PROMPTS.json, selected source PNGs in sources/, and website JPEG derivatives in public/images/. Original photographs stay outside the website and repository. JPEG sources were converted to correctly oriented PNG inputs for compatibility with imagegen; no creative reference editing was performed outside imagegen.

The campaign uses soft window light, ivory limestone and a consistent warm studio grade. The category banner keeps the existing curved composition and positions the oxblood product on the right. AI images are visual styling aids and do not verify dimensions, materials, stock or authenticity. Raw phone photographs and packaging are not served by the site. Visible AI/demo labels were removed at the owner's request; provenance remains documented here. Checkout remains non-transactional.

Product galleries currently provide a full image and a clearly named detail crop of that same image. They do not claim an independent rear or side photograph. Each listing carousel stays within its default variant's images; it never rotates through different colourways.
