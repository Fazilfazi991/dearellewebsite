# Asset provenance
The current hero delivery uses versioned `-v2` H.264 exports and opening-frame WebP/JPEG posters generated from the same approved masters. No creative film edit was made. `media/hero/optimize.mjs` reproduces them; `media/hero/PERFORMANCE.md` records encoding and measurements. Local WOFF2 files were converted from the supplied TTFs with unchanged glyphs and horizontal metrics; existing font licensing applies.

The Option 2 category and editorial photographs are fictional, unbranded demo concepts generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the user-approved layout as a composition reference. Original PNGs and the exact prompt set are retained in `media/option2/`; responsive site sources are `public/images/option2-watch.jpg`, `option2-bag.jpg` and `option2-editorial.jpg`. JPEGs were encoded with ffmpeg and have embedded prompt provenance. They do not represent verified Dearelle inventory. The first two demo product cards reuse these assets; other catalogue photographs remain representative stock.

Catalogue and moodboard images are representative stock photographs from Unsplash, downloaded locally. They do not show Dearelle products or customers and confer no brand affiliation. Replace with verified owned product photography before launch. Product colour names are demo interaction data and do not promise pictured variants.

The current hero uses three actual Google Flow Veo source takes, edited locally into 9:16 and 16:9 films. Files and generation/credit provenance are in `media/hero/PRODUCTION.md`; website MP4s and posters are in `public/videos/`. These are concept accessories, not verified inventory. The user requested no visible concept label or campaign title over the film.

`public/brand/dearelle-logo.png` is the original transparent gold logo supplied by the user, copied with its pixels unchanged and used in the header/footer. It is a real supplied brand asset, not stock photography.
